### Measure
- [x] Ablation A: pixel loss against pixel + Sinkhorn, 3 seeds, 500 strokes, oil brush, 5x5 grid, original light renderer, 30 images (180 runs, `experiments/2026-10-03_ablation_a_pixel_vs_sinkhorn`): PSNR 18.67 against 18.69 dB, SSIM 0.490 for both, paired PSNR difference +0.011 dB (95 % CI -0.027 to +0.049, Wilcoxon p 0.98, n = 30 images), steps to 22 dB 335 for both; the Sinkhorn term costs 2.6 times the optimization time (6.17 s against 15.99 s). ### Notes and deviations
- **The unit of the paired statistics is the image**, not the run: the three seeds of an image are averaged first (report equation 4.2). This is stricter than the plan's "mean and standard deviation over 3 seeds", which is still what the headline table cells show.
- **Items added to the plan** because the first results asked for them: the comparison with the original code, the gradient diagnostic, Ablation A2 (starting positions) and Ablation A3 (the 3x3 dilate / erode step). The last one found the largest effect of any component tested here after the grid (2.71 dB).
- **The weight and epsilon sweeps use subsets** (10 and 5 of the 30 images, every third and every sixth image of the sorted set) to fit the time; their intervals are about +-0.1 and +-0.2 dB wide. The epsilon sweep was run at weight 100 because at the reference weight the term does nothing.
- **Where this package deliberately differs from the original**: it averages when it shrinks the target (`INTER_AREA`, +0.32 dB), the original uses bilinear. `PainterConfig.resize = "linear"` reproduces the original, and with it the two agree (-0.02 dB).
- **CUDA runs are not bit-reproducible** unless `torch.backends.cudnn.deterministic` is set (`--deterministic` of the experiment scripts, `core.device.set_deterministic`; verified: identical strokes). The Week 5 experiments were run without it: a repeated (image, config, seed) cell differs by 0.10 dB at the median, up to 0.85 dB, so conclusions rest on averages. Suggested: make it the default from Week 6 on, and use it for the final Week 9 evaluation.
- **Timings moved by a factor of two between runs on the same desktop** (full-image mode 36 s early in the afternoon, 74 s in the evening; the final rasterization 1.4 s and 2.7 s), probably the processor's state. Only ratios measured together mean much. The cost of the Sinkhorn term is 2.3 to 2.6 times.
- **The marker pen cannot start with the original's initialization** (see "Weeks 6 to 10"): Week 6 item.
- **Machine limits met**: more than two PyTorch CUDA processes at once exhausted Windows commit memory (a sweep stopped with a NumPy allocation error and was resumed), and the tests must not run while a timed experiment is running.

### Notes and deviations
- **Windows power throttling.** The largest speed effect of the week was not in the code: Windows 11 throttles a busy background process (EcoQoS). It explains the "factor of two over a day" of Weeks 5 and 6, whose timings are upper bounds. Every script now opts out; `profile_step.py` reproduces the effect.
- **Pipeline defaults live in `run_paint.py`.** `PainterConfig` keeps the reference's defaults (constant 0.002, equal allocation, float32), so older experiments stay reproducible from their recorded settings; `run_ablation.py` configs write the pipeline's settings out (`mode=progressive,lr_schedule=cosine,lr=0.004`).
- **Equal split at small budgets.** The reference's split paints only 55 of 100 strokes (495 of 500); the scaling study reports strokes painted. `allocation="error"` paints the exact budget.
- **Timings.** Quality runs were made two at a time; every time in the report comes from runs made alone (three images, seed 0). Full-renderer times varied up to 5x between two runs of the same painting (memory did not); they are not reported as measurements.
- **Renderers.** All Week 7 runs used the authors' light renderers (the Week 6 decision on the retrained Bezier renderers is still the student's; the pipeline can take either with `--renderer`).
